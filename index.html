Where does your authority stand?

Confidence and composure are a function of authority — and authority grows from five capacities: Confidence, Leadership, Enjoyment, Gratitude and Discipline. This inventory measures each of them across the seven areas that make up a life.

How it works

  • 35 quick ratings — 5 statements for each of 7 life areas. About 8–10 minutes.
  • Rate each statement 1 (not true of me) to 10 (completely true of me).
  • Answer for how things actually are — not how you think they should be. It's private; nothing is sent anywhere.
